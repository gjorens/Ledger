First use

Create your first account (you can add more later), or open an existing ledger file above.

Recurring items

The amount here is an estimate. When the biller tells you the real amount, press Edit on that month's projected row to confirm it (it then shows in bold). If you know the amount will change from a certain date, use Change amount. If the first date is in the past, those occurrences show as Due so you can post or skip them.

Monthly reconciliation

Tick each item that appears on the statement. Items not ticked can be edited here and carry over to the next month if they stay unticked.

Reports

Transfers between your own accounts, deleted entries and projected items are left out.
